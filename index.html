How it works

Three layers, stacked. Everything is free and runs entirely in your browser — no account, no API key, no limits.

  • 🖼️ Main image — the scene at the bottom of the stack. Give it its own exposure and colour.
  • 🧍 Subject — cut out in the Cut Out tab, saved to your Library, then tapped into the scene. Move / resize / rotate it, refine the cut edge, ground it with a contact shadow, and hit Match subject to the scene to harmonize its light and colour.
  • 🎞️ Overlay — a texture photo blended over the top (soft light, screen, multiply…) to carry the whole composite into one feel.
  • ✨ Finish — vignette, grain and a faded-black film curve over the flattened result.

Every layer has an eye toggle so you can check its contribution. Auto edits set all of it in one tap — then move any slider you like. First background removal downloads the model once (cached afterward). Your photos never leave your device.

How it works

This app is completely free and runs entirely in your browser — there's no account, no API key, and no usage limits.

  • ✂️ Cut Out — pick a photo and tap Remove background. A small AI model runs on your device to erase the background and leave a transparent cutout. Save it to your Library.
  • 🎬 Scene — pick a base image, tap cutouts to drop them in, then move / resize / rotate them. Turn on cast shadows and pick a color grade, then Merge to flatten it into a finished image.
  • 📚 Library — your saved cutouts stay in this browser between visits.

First background removal downloads the model once (cached afterward), so it needs a connection that first time. Everything is processed locally — your photos never leave your device.
